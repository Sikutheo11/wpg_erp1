# 1. Purpose

This document defines every business workflow inside the WPG ERP.

Every module must follow these workflows.

No implementation should violate these business processes.

# 5. INVENTORY WORKFLOW

Inventory controls all stock.

No stock changes outside Inventory.

---

## Stock IN

Generated by:

- Production Output
- Purchases
- Adjustments
- Transfers

```text
Production

↓

Stock Movement (IN)

↓

Inventory Updated
```

---

## Stock OUT

Generated by:

- Ecommerce Sale
- Walk-in Sale
- Customer Delivery
- Adjustment
- Transfer

```text
Customer Purchase

↓

Stock Movement (OUT)

↓

Inventory Updated
```

# 20. BUSINESS RULES

## Rule 1

Every product enters Inventory before sale.

---

## Rule 2

Every sale reduces Inventory.

---

## Rule 3

Every financial transaction reaches Finance.

---

## Rule 4

Production never performs sales.

---

## Rule 5

Inventory is the only source of stock quantity.

---

## Rule 6

Customer custom products become Finished Goods before delivery.

---

## Rule 7

All workflows must remain traceable.
