# WISDOM PALACE GROUP CONSTITUTION

# 01 — PREAMBLE

---

> **"An institution without principles will eventually lose its purpose.  
A business without purpose will eventually lose its people.  
A people without integrity will eventually lose their future."**

---

## PREAMBLE

Wisdom Palace Group (WPG) was founded upon the conviction that business is one of the most powerful instruments for creating sustainable prosperity, restoring human dignity, and transforming society.

We believe that businesses should exist not merely to generate profit, but to create opportunities, solve meaningful problems, develop people, strengthen communities, and leave a legacy that endures beyond the lives of their founders.

This Constitution represents the collective philosophy upon which Wisdom Palace Group shall be governed.

It defines who we are.

Why we exist.

What we believe.

How we make decisions.

How we serve people.

How we manage resources.

How we build trust.

And how we prepare future generations to continue this institution.

---

## OUR COMMITMENT

We therefore commit ourselves to build an organization that shall always be guided by:

- Integrity before convenience.
- Transparency before secrecy.
- Long-term value before short-term profit.
- Excellence before mediocrity.
- Service before self-interest.
- Stewardship before ownership.
- Innovation before complacency.
- Learning before pride.

These principles shall remain the permanent foundation upon which every policy, every process, every investment, every technology, every business unit and every strategic decision of Wisdom Palace Group shall be built.

---

## OUR RESPONSIBILITY

We recognize that every organization possesses responsibilities toward many stakeholders.

Therefore WPG commits itself to creating lasting value for:

- Customers, through exceptional products and services.
- Employees, through meaningful work, continuous development and shared prosperity.
- Investors, through responsible stewardship, transparency and sustainable growth.
- Communities, through ethical business practices and social impact.
- Future generations, by building institutions capable of enduring beyond the present.

---

## OUR UNDERSTANDING OF SUCCESS

At Wisdom Palace Group, success shall never be measured by financial performance alone.

True success shall be measured by our ability to:

- Build trustworthy institutions.
- Create sustainable employment.
- Develop future leaders.
- Earn lasting customer confidence.
- Deliver operational excellence.
- Generate responsible profits.
- Protect the environment.
- Strengthen our communities.
- Inspire innovation.
- Leave a legacy worthy of future generations.

Profit remains essential.

But profit shall never replace purpose.

Growth remains important.

But growth shall never replace integrity.

---

## OUR RESPONSIBILITY TO THE FUTURE

We acknowledge that the decisions made today will influence generations yet unborn.

For this reason, every leader entrusted with responsibility within Wisdom Palace Group shall act not merely as an owner of today's resources, but as a steward of tomorrow's opportunities.

We therefore reject decisions motivated solely by personal interest, short-term gain, or temporary advantage.

Instead, we commit ourselves to building systems, culture, leadership, knowledge and institutions that shall continue creating value long after today's leaders have completed their service.

---

## OUR STRATEGIC COMMITMENT (2026–2035)

During the first strategic phase of Wisdom Palace Group, the organization shall focus on strengthening three core business units:

- Furniture & Manufacturing
- Construction & Built Environment
- Agriculture (Poultry)

Supported by two strategic enterprise platforms:

- WPG Business Operating System (ERP)
- WPG Marketplace

All future expansion shall be evaluated according to the principles established in this Constitution and shall only proceed when it strengthens, rather than weakens, the long-term sustainability of the institution.

---

## OUR COVENANT

Every employee.

Every manager.

Every executive.

Every board member.

Every investor.

Every strategic partner.

Who chooses to become part of Wisdom Palace Group accepts the responsibility of protecting the principles established within this Constitution.

No position.

No authority.

No opportunity.

No financial benefit.

Shall ever justify compromising the values upon which WPG is built.

---

## THE FOUNDING DECLARATION

From this day forward,

Wisdom Palace Group shall exist to build enduring businesses that create lasting opportunities for people.

We shall pursue excellence with humility.

We shall pursue profit with integrity.

We shall pursue growth with discipline.

We shall pursue innovation with responsibility.

And above all,

**We shall always choose to build an institution that future generations will be proud to inherit.**
