# CHAPTER EIGHT

# THE WPG OPERATING MODEL

> **"Excellence is not achieved by working harder. It is achieved by designing better systems."**

---

# 8.1 Purpose

The WPG Operating Model defines how Wisdom Palace Group creates value.

It establishes the operating principles, workflows, organizational interactions and enterprise processes that enable every Business Unit to function as one integrated institution.

The Operating Model exists to ensure that:

- Every customer receives consistent service.
- Every employee understands their role.
- Every department collaborates effectively.
- Every business process is measurable.
- Every decision supports institutional excellence.

---

# 8.2 One Enterprise Philosophy

Although WPG operates multiple Business Units, the institution shall always function as **One Enterprise**.

Furniture & Manufacturing.

Construction & Built Environment.

Agriculture (Poultry).

Strategic Platforms.

All shall pursue one mission.

All shall follow one Constitution.

All shall operate under one governance framework.

Success in one Business Unit strengthens the entire institution.

Failure in one Business Unit affects the entire institution.

Therefore collaboration shall always prevail over competition.

---

---

# 8.4 Shared Services Model

Certain institutional functions shall serve all Business Units equally.

These Shared Services include:

- Finance & Treasury
- Human Resources
- Procurement
- Legal & Compliance
- Enterprise Technology
- Internal Audit
- Marketing & Brand
- Corporate Communications
- Strategy & Business Development

Shared Services exist to eliminate duplication, reduce costs and strengthen consistency across the institution.

---

# 8.5 Business Unit Independence

Each Core Business Unit shall maintain operational responsibility for:

Daily Operations.

Customer Relationships.

Production.

Project Execution.

Revenue Generation.

Operational Planning.

Innovation within its domain.

However, institutional policies remain common across all Business Units.

---

# 8.6 Decision-Making Framework

Every decision shall be made at the lowest competent level.

Employees shall make operational decisions.

Managers shall make departmental decisions.

Executives shall make strategic decisions.

The Board shall make institutional decisions.

Authority shall never exceed accountability.

---

# 8.7 Approval Framework

Every significant business activity shall follow an approved authorization process.

Examples include:

- Procurement
- Payments
- Hiring
- Salary Adjustments
- Capital Investments
- Contracts
- Asset Disposal
- Strategic Partnerships

Approval levels shall be defined by policy and supported by the WPG Business Operating System.

---

# 8.8 Process Standardization

Every recurring business activity shall have a documented Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).

No critical process shall depend solely upon personal knowledge.

Documentation protects institutional continuity.

---

# 8.9 Operational Excellence

Operational Excellence shall be measured through:

Quality.

Productivity.

Customer Satisfaction.

Lead Time.

Cost Efficiency.

Safety.

Innovation.

Continuous Improvement.

Every Business Unit shall continuously monitor these indicators.

---

# 8.10 Risk Management

Operational risks shall be identified before they become crises.

Every department shall maintain awareness of:

Operational Risks.

Financial Risks.

Safety Risks.

Technology Risks.

Supply Chain Risks.

Reputation Risks.

Business Continuity Risks.

Risk Management is everyone's responsibility.

---

# 8.11 Knowledge Management

Knowledge generated within WPG belongs to the institution.

Every lesson learned.

Every improvement.

Every innovation.

Every documented procedure.

Every project experience.

Shall become institutional knowledge available for future generations.

Knowledge shall never leave with individuals.

---

# 8.12 Performance Management

Performance shall be evaluated at four levels:

Individual Performance.

Department Performance.

Business Unit Performance.

Enterprise Performance.

Success shall be measured using both:

Operational Results.

Institutional Values.

Neither shall exist without the other.

---

# 8.13 Continuous Improvement Cycle

WPG adopts a permanent improvement cycle.

Plan

↓

Execute

↓

Measure

↓

Review

↓

Improve

↓

Standardize

↓

Repeat

Improvement is a continuous responsibility rather than an occasional initiative.

---

# 8.14 Enterprise Integration

Every Business Unit shall exchange information through the WPG Business Operating System.

No department shall maintain isolated information where enterprise integration is required.

Information belongs to the institution.

Not to departments.

---

# 8.15 Scalability

The Operating Model shall support future expansion.

Future companies.

Future countries.

Future products.

Future technologies.

Future institutions.

Growth shall strengthen the institution rather than increase unnecessary complexity.

---

# 8.16 The Operating Covenant

Every employee shall remember:

The quality of our systems determines the quality of our institution.

The quality of our institution determines the quality of our future.

Therefore every process deserves continuous improvement.

Every customer deserves operational excellence.

Every employee deserves clear systems.

Every leader deserves reliable information.

Every generation deserves a stronger institution than the one it inherited.

---

# Reflection for Leaders

Before improving any process, ask:

- Does this simplify work?
- Does it reduce waste?
- Does it increase transparency?
- Does it improve customer experience?
- Does it strengthen the institution?
- Can this process continue even if key individuals leave?

---

Institutions endure when systems continuously improve."**
