## Official Declaration

This Constitution establishes the principles, philosophy, governance, operating model, and long-term direction of Wisdom Palace Group (WPG).

It serves as the supreme guiding document for every business unit, every employee, every leader, every investor, every decision, every process, and every future generation entrusted with the stewardship of WPG.

All corporate policies, procedures, operating manuals, software systems, business strategies, and governance frameworks shall align with the principles established within this Constitution.

## The WPG Promise

Wisdom Palace Group exists for a purpose greater than profit.

We believe that great businesses create value before creating wealth.

They strengthen families.

They build communities.

They develop people.

They inspire innovation.

They earn trust.

They leave lasting legacies.

Everything we build shall therefore honor these commitments.
