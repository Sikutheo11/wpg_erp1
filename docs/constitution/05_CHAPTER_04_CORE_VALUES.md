> **"Values are not words written on walls.

# 4.1 Our Values

The strength of Wisdom Palace Group shall never depend solely on its products, technology or financial resources.

Its greatest strength shall always be its values.

These values define how we think.

How we work.

How we lead.

How we serve.

How we make decisions.

Every employee, manager, executive, board member and strategic partner is expected to live these values every day.

---

# VALUE ONE

# Integrity

## We Believe

Integrity is doing the right thing even when no one is watching.

Trust cannot exist without integrity.

Every reputation is built one decision at a time.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Speak truthfully.
- Keep our promises.
- Honor agreements.
- Protect company resources.
- Reject corruption.
- Reject fraud.
- Admit mistakes.
- Correct errors quickly.

---

### We Never

- Manipulate records.
- Mislead customers.
- Hide mistakes.
- Accept bribery.
- Abuse company property.

---

# VALUE TWO

# Transparency

## We Believe

Transparency creates trust.

Trust creates lasting institutions.

Every important business decision should leave evidence.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Maintain complete audit trails.
- Document decisions.
- Report honestly.
- Share accurate information.
- Protect accountability.

---

### We Never

- Conceal important information.
- Create false reports.
- Hide financial transactions.
- Bypass approvals.

---

# VALUE THREE

# Excellence

## We Believe

Good enough is never enough.

Everything carrying the WPG name should reflect excellence.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Improve continuously.
- Deliver quality.
- Respect deadlines.
- Learn every day.
- Finish what we start.

---

### We Never

- Accept avoidable errors.
- Ignore customer complaints.
- Stop learning.
- Lower standards.

---

# VALUE FOUR

# Stewardship

## We Believe

We do not own WPG.

We are entrusted to protect it for future generations.

Every resource must be managed responsibly.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Protect company assets.
- Use resources wisely.
- Think long-term.
- Prepare future leaders.
- Build sustainable systems.

---

### We Never

- Waste resources.
- Pursue short-term gains that damage the institution.
- Abuse authority.

---

# VALUE FIVE

# Innovation

## We Believe

Standing still means falling behind.

Innovation is everyone's responsibility.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Improve products.
- Improve processes.
- Improve technology.
- Encourage ideas.
- Learn from mistakes.

---

### We Never

- Resist positive change.
- Reject better ideas because of pride.
- Punish responsible experimentation.

---

# VALUE SIX

# Respect for People

## We Believe

Every person possesses dignity.

Respect is not determined by position.

Every employee deserves fairness.

Every customer deserves honesty.

Every supplier deserves professionalism.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Listen respectfully.
- Treat everyone fairly.
- Develop people.
- Celebrate diversity of ideas.
- Build teamwork.

---

### We Never

- Humiliate others.
- Discriminate.
- Abuse authority.
- Harass employees.
- Disrespect customers.

---

# VALUE SEVEN

# Accountability

## We Believe

Responsibility cannot exist without accountability.

Every decision has an owner.

Every task has an outcome.

Every outcome teaches a lesson.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Accept responsibility.
- Deliver results.
- Learn from failure.
- Measure performance.
- Improve continuously.

---

### We Never

- Blame others.
- Avoid responsibility.
- Ignore performance.
- Repeat preventable mistakes.

---

# VALUE EIGHT

# Customer Success

## We Believe

Our success depends entirely upon the success of our customers.

We therefore exist to create value before seeking profit.

---

### We therefore commit to:

- Listen carefully.
- Deliver quality.
- Keep our promises.
- Solve customer problems.
- Build long-term relationships.

---

### We Never

- Mislead customers.
- Sell poor quality.
- Ignore complaints.
- Break commitments.

---

# Living Our Values

These values shall guide:

- Recruitment.
- Promotion.
- Performance Evaluation.
- Leadership Selection.
- Compensation.
- Recognition.
- Strategic Decision Making.

Competence without values shall never qualify a person for leadership within Wisdom Palace Group.

---

# Reflection for Leaders

Before proceeding to the next chapter, every leader should ask:

- Which of these values did I demonstrate today?
- Which value needs greater attention in my leadership?
- Would my team describe me as a model of these values?
- Are my decisions strengthening the culture we seek to build?
