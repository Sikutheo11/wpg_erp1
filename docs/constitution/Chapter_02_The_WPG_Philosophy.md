# BOOK ONE --- THE FOUNDATION

## CHAPTER 2 --- THE WPG PHILOSOPHY

> Businesses do not fail because they lack opportunities. They fail
> because they lack principles.

### 2.1 What We Believe About Business

Business exists to solve meaningful problems before seeking financial
reward.

**Money follows value. Value never follows money.**

### 2.2 What We Believe About Wealth

WPG recognizes five forms of capital:

-   Financial Capital
-   Human Capital
-   Intellectual Capital
-   Social Capital
-   Institutional Capital

### 2.3 What We Believe About Profit

Profit is not our mission. Profit is our responsibility.

### 2.4 What We Believe About People

People are not expenses. People are creators of value.

### 2.5 What We Believe About Leadership

Leadership is stewardship.

### 2.6 What We Believe About Customers

We earn customer trust every day.

### 2.7 What We Believe About Investors

Investors are partners in our mission.

### 2.8 What We Believe About Innovation

Innovation is a habit.

### 2.9 What We Believe About Transparency

Every important business decision shall leave evidence.

### 2.10 What We Believe About Time

Time is more valuable than money.

### 2.11 What We Believe About the Future

We shall never sacrifice tomorrow for today's convenience.

## The WPG Oath

We do not inherit WPG from our founders. We borrow it from future
generations.
