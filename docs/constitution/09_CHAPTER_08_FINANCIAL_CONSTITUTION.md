# CHAPTER SEVEN

# THE PEOPLE CONSTITUTION

> **"Great institutions are never built by buildings, machines or money. They are built by people who share a common purpose."**

---

# 7.1 Our Belief About People

At Wisdom Palace Group, people are not costs to be minimized.

People are the institution's greatest investment.

Machines increase productivity.

Technology increases efficiency.

Capital creates opportunities.

But only people create lasting value.

For this reason, every person who joins WPG shall be regarded as a builder of the institution rather than merely an employee.

---

# 7.2 The Purpose of Employment

Employment at WPG exists for two purposes.

First,

To create value for customers and society.

Second,

To develop the individual.

Every employee should leave WPG stronger than when they arrived.

Better skilled.

Better disciplined.

Better prepared.

Better able to lead.

Better able to serve.

If an employee works at WPG for many years without personal growth,

The institution has failed one of its responsibilities.

---

# 7.3 Equal Opportunity

Wisdom Palace Group believes that opportunity should be determined by:

Character.

Competence.

Commitment.

Contribution.

Not by:

Family connections.

Friendship.

Tribe.

Religion.

Political affiliation.

Gender.

Personal influence.

Every individual shall receive equal opportunity to grow according to merit.

---

# 7.4 Recruitment Philosophy

We hire for:

Character first.

Attitude second.

Competence third.

Skills can be taught.

Integrity cannot.

Therefore,

No amount of technical ability shall compensate for lack of integrity.

---

# 7.5 Continuous Development

Every employee shall receive opportunities for:

Professional training.

Leadership development.

Technical improvement.

Financial literacy.

Innovation.

Personal growth.

Learning is considered part of work—not separate from work.

---

# 7.6 Leadership Development

Every leader has one permanent responsibility:

To prepare future leaders.

Leadership is not measured by followers.

Leadership is measured by successors.

Every manager shall mentor others.

Every executive shall develop future executives.

Every generation shall prepare the next.

---

# 7.7 Performance and Values

Performance matters.

Values matter more.

Promotion shall never depend solely upon results.

Leadership positions shall require both:

Outstanding performance.

Faithful demonstration of WPG Values.

A person who achieves results by violating institutional values shall not be considered successful.

---

# 7.8 Shared Prosperity

As Wisdom Palace Group grows,

Its people should grow with it.

The institution shall establish long-term systems that enable employees to participate in the prosperity they help create.

Such systems may include:

Performance Bonuses.

Profit Sharing.

Employee Investment Programs.

Share Ownership Plans.

Leadership Incentives.

Retirement Programs.

Housing Support.

Education Support.

The specific programs may evolve over time.

The principle shall never change.

Growth should benefit those who help create it.

---

# 7.9 Employee Ownership Philosophy

At WPG,

Ownership is earned through contribution.

The institution believes that individuals who consistently create value should have opportunities to build long-term wealth alongside the organization.

Ownership shall therefore be based upon:

Integrity.

Performance.

Years of contribution.

Leadership.

Institutional impact.

Never merely upon position.

---

# 7.10 Employee Wellbeing

WPG recognizes that sustainable performance requires healthy people.

The institution therefore commits itself to promoting:

Physical wellbeing.

Mental wellbeing.

Financial wellbeing.

Professional wellbeing.

Family wellbeing.

Healthy people build healthy institutions.

---

# 7.11 Discipline

Discipline protects excellence.

Every employee shall be accountable for:

Conduct.

Performance.

Attendance.

Integrity.

Safety.

Respect.

Discipline shall always be:

Fair.

Consistent.

Respectful.

Documented.

Designed to restore whenever possible.

---

# 7.12 Recognition

People flourish when excellence is recognized.

WPG shall celebrate:

Innovation.

Integrity.

Leadership.

Service.

Learning.

Teamwork.

Customer Impact.

Recognition strengthens culture.

---

# 7.13 The WPG Employee Promise

Every person who joins Wisdom Palace Group should be able to say:

"I became a better professional because I worked at WPG."

"I became a better leader because I worked at WPG."

"I became a better person because I worked at WPG."

If WPG consistently fulfills this promise,

The institution will never struggle to attract great people.

---

# 7.14 The WPG Covenant with Employees

Wisdom Palace Group promises every employee:

We shall lead with integrity.

We shall treat you fairly.

We shall invest in your growth.

We shall recognize your contribution.

We shall protect your dignity.

We shall build systems that enable your success.

In return,

Every employee commits to:

Protect the institution.

Serve customers with excellence.

Live the WPG Values.

Continue learning.

Support fellow employees.

Leave WPG stronger than they found it.

---

# Reflection for Leaders

Before leading others, ask yourself:

- Am I developing people or merely supervising them?
- If my team leaves me tomorrow, what knowledge have I transferred?
- Would I want my own child to work under my leadership?
- Am I building employees or future leaders?

---

> **"The greatest investment of Wisdom Palace Group is not its buildings, machines or technology.

Its greatest investment is people.

And the greatest responsibility of leadership is to help people become more than they believed they could become."**
