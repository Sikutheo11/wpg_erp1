# WPG BOS — ERP Master Architecture

Version: 1.0 Draft

## Purpose

WPG BOS is the Business Operating System of Wisdom Palace Group.  
It exists to connect governance, operations, finance, people, inventory, production, construction, agriculture, marketplace and reporting into one enterprise platform.

## Architecture

WPG BOS is composed of six major layers:

1. Core Engine
2. Master Data
3. Shared Services
4. Business Units
5. Platforms
6. Intelligence

## 1. Core Engine

- Accounts / Authentication
- RBAC / Roles / Permissions
- Workflow Engine
- Approval Engine
- Notification Engine
- Audit Trail
- Document Management

## 2. Master Data

- Business Units
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Employees
- Products
- Warehouses
- Assets
- Projects

## 3. Shared Services

- Finance
- Treasury
- HR / Payroll
- Inventory
- Procurement
- CRM
- Assets

## 4. Business Units

- Furniture & Manufacturing
- Construction & Built Environment
- Agriculture / Poultry

## 5. Platforms

- WPG Marketplace
- Customer Portal
- Employee Portal
- Supplier Portal
- Investor Portal

## 6. Intelligence

- Dashboards
- Reports
- KPI Engine
- Forecasting
- AI
